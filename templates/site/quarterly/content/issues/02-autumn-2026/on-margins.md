The grid was rigid, and the margins were wide.[^1] Every page of the 1971 Brno catalogue left a third of its width empty,{>> cf. Gerstner, *Designing Programmes*, 1964 <<} and the emptiness was not waste.

A footnote sends the reader down and back up again.[^2] A margin note stays beside the line it belongs to,{>> Tufte's sidenotes are the modern descendant. <<} so the eye only moves sideways.

Příliš žluťoučký kůň úpěl ďábelské ódy, as the type specimens of the period liked to say.[^3] The sentence tests every diacritic a Czech text needs, and it tested the margins too: a long accented line is the first to run into them.{>> Polish specimens did the same with *Zażółć gęślą jaźń*. <<}

[^1]: Brno, 1971.
[^2]: Like this one.
[^3]: "Too yellow a horse moaned devilish odes."
