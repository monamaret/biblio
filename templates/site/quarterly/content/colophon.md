*The Quarterly* is published four times a year, in numbered issues.

Text is set in **Archivo**, by Omnibus-Type; folios and labels in **IBM Plex Mono**, by IBM. Both are used under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, whose texts ship with this site in `/fonts/`.

Images are printed as black-and-white plates. The site is built with biblio.
